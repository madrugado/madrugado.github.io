<p class="lead">I am an NLP researcher. I lead NLP research at <a href="https://mts.ai">MWS AI</a> and am a Research Professor at the <a href="https://iitu.edu.kz">International IT University</a> in Almaty, Kazakhstan. I also work on research projects with <a href="https://en.itmo.ru/">ITMO University</a>.</p>

My main field is **natural language generation**: summarization, large language models and their evaluation, code generation and LLM agents, and NLP for Russian, Kazakh and other lower-resource languages. Since modern NLG is built on deep learning, deep learning is my second area of interest.

If you are a student looking for a thesis supervisor, see the [teaching page](/teaching/).

## Background
- **PhD** (2019) — thesis defended at the Institute for Systems Programming, Russian Academy of Sciences. [Thesis and synopsis](https://www.ispras.ru/dcouncil/docs/diss/2019/malyh/malyh.php) (in Russian); a short English version was [published at ACL 2019](https://aclanthology.org/P19-2002/).
- **MSc in Computer Science** (2009) — Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology.

Before MWS AI I worked as a Senior Research Scientist at Huawei Noah's Ark Lab, an Applied Research Scientist at VK, an ML engineer at Yandex, and a Research Scientist at the Neural Systems and Deep Learning Lab of MIPT, where I took part in building the [DeepPavlov](https://deeppavlov.ai) library and organizing the ConvAI challenges. Earlier I also worked on computer vision for robotics at Cognitive Technologies.

My publications are listed [here](/publications/) and on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Q-DWwNAAAAAJ). If a paper has no link, just email me.
